About the festival

Grow human capital by bringing young people into STEM

Alem Tech Fest is the international festival of USTEM Foundation, the official FIRST operator for Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan. In 2027 the festival moves to the new campus of the Kazakh National University of Sports in Astana.

Teams aged 4 to 25 and older bring robots, drones, satellites and code. Results count toward national rankings and qualification to international championships.

CompeteFIRST, RoboCup, drones, space, cybersecurity
LearnWorkshops, Hour of AI and an open expo zone
ConnectTeams from Central Asia and beyond

Venue

Kazakh National University of Sports

A new campus in Astana, opened in July 2026: a glass-roofed atrium, an indoor athletics arena, sports halls, a conference stage and an outdoor stadium. All competition zones are under one roof.

Getting there

Fly into Nursultan Nazarbayev International Airport (NQZ). Taxi apps (Yandex Go, inDrive) work across the city.

Weather in February

Daytime is usually around −10 °C, nights often drop below −20 °C, and it is windy. Bring a warm coat, hat and gloves.
